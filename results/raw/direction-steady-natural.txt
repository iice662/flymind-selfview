definitions:
  w_j      = (net input to CX cell j from side A) - (net input from side B), net = E - I
  D(dir)   = sum_j w_j * rate_j(dir)
  index    = (D(right) - D(left)) / (|D(right)| + |D(left)|)
  corr(dr,w) = Pearson r over cells between (rate_j(right) - rate_j(left)) and w_j
  corr(R,L)  = Pearson r over cells between the rate vector in the right-turn state and the
               rate vector in the left-turn state (negative = the pattern flips with direction)
  DSI      = mean over active cells of (r_right - r_left) / (r_right + r_left)
