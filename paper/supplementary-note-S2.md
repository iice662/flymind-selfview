## S2.2 How this log was assembled

The work was directed as a sequence of chat turns in one session. This note groups the
instructions by the phase of work each one initiated and reproduces them in the language in which
they were issued (Chinese), each with an English gloss and the artifacts it produced.

Two marking conventions, stated so the record is not overstated:

- Entries marked **[as recorded]** are reproduced from the session record of that turn; where the
  record itself elides text the elision is shown as `…`.
- Entries marked **[recorded form]** are instructions whose exact wording is no longer recoverable
  from the session record; the substance of the request is given as it was recorded. The authors
  retain the original session transcript and can paste exact wording here in its place.

No instruction in this note was reworded to make the work look more independent of the tool than it
was: the instructions that determined the circuit, the manipulation, the statistics and the claims
are all reproduced below.

*Gloss:* using the complete *Drosophila* connectome, demonstrate computationally, for the first

## S2.4 Experiment harness, drive model and balancing

**S2.4.1 The three methodological requirements.** **[as recorded]**

> 「阳性对照：证明系统能被扰动。信号传播验证：证明第三人称信号确实到达了目标回路。等效性检验：用贝叶斯因子或等效检验支持"无差异"。」

*Gloss:* positive controls — show the system can be perturbed; signal-propagation verification —
show the third-person signal actually reaches the target circuit; equivalence testing — use Bayes
factors or equivalence tests to support "no difference".
→ Results, *Signal propagation*, *Positive controls*; Fig. 2; Fig. S1C, S1D; Tables 1–2;
Fig. S2 (equivalence bounds); Fig. S3 (Bayes-factor design ceiling); Methods, *Statistics*.

→ the per-step drive-matching scheme (eq. S1); `DriveProfile` in `tools/CircuitPreview/Experiment.cs`;
Fig. 1D–1F; Results, *The drive-matching trap*; Fig. S4.

## S2.5 Analysis and reporting constraints

**S2.5.1 Raw results, no summary.** **[as recorded]**

> 「直接把结果给我不要帮我总结什么的，我得写类似研究不让ai写图表和总结」

*Gloss:* give me the results directly, do not summarise them for me; I have to write research of
this kind, and I do not want AI-written figure text or summaries.

**S2.5.2 Numbers, not adjectives.** **[as recorded]**

> 「给数字比较不要主观评价」

*Gloss:* give numeric comparisons, not subjective evaluations.
→ these two instructions govern the register of the Results section and of every figure annotation:
effect sizes, confidence intervals, test statistics and P values only; no editorial adjectives.
